## Abstract

We present NIL33, a deterministic underwriting engine for structured securities backed by collegiate athlete Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) revenue streams. The system introduces a 33-signal, six-dimension scoring ontology that maps observable athlete characteristics to composite creditworthiness grades through pure, side-effect-free functions. A cryptographic model fingerprint—the *Genome Signature*—captures seven canonical components (signal schema, weight profile, grade thresholds, stress matrix, covenant rules, flag rules, valuation model) in a single 128-bit identifier derived from SHA-256 hashing, ensuring bit-exact output reproduction given identical inputs. We describe the signal taxonomy, instrument-sensitive dimension weighting, automated covenant generation, multi-scenario stress testing, seeded Monte Carlo Value-at-Risk with correlated dimension shocks, and the genome identity system. We then present two extensions absent from prior work: (1) lifecycle integration, in which every underwriting run, instrument, and distribution object is stamped with its originating genome version, enabling provenance tracing across the capital lifecycle; and (2) portfolio genome analytics, which quantify model homogeneity via the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index, detect pairwise genome drift, and score mutation risk across a book of positions. The engine is implemented in 3,556 lines of strict-mode TypeScript with 75 automated tests, zero runtime dependencies beyond SHA-256, and a research snapshot exporter that packages the full model specification—including synthetic verification vectors—for DOI registration and peer review. To our knowledge, this is the first published deterministic underwriting framework for athlete-backed structured finance with formal model versioning, capital object provenance, and portfolio-level evolutionary analytics.
